# The Knowing-Doing Gap Skill

This skill captures key principles, diagnostic mental models, and actionable leadership wisdom distilled from *The Knowing-Doing Gap: How Smart Companies Turn Knowledge into Action* by Jeffrey Pfeffer and Robert I. Sutton (Harvard Business School Press). 

It operates as a **Reading Companion & Note Capture Assistant**, an **Organizational Diagnostic Advisor**, and a **Socratic Sparring Partner (Adaptive 5-Question Quizzer)**.

## Trigger Conditions

Activate this skill when:
1. **Note Dictation & Reading Updates**: The user is reading *The Knowing-Doing Gap* and wants to dictate reflections, capture principles, or summarize key sections into the knowledge base (e.g., *"Capture this insight from Chapter 2"*, *"Add this note to my Knowing-Doing Gap notes"*).
2. **Concept Clarification**: The user asks to explore or clarify mechanisms, company case studies, or counter-intuitive management principles from the book.
3. **Organizational Execution Diagnosis**: The user wants to diagnose why a team, initiative, or organization is stalled: analyzing whether talk has substituted for action, fear has choked risk-taking, or metrics have distorted focus.
4. **Interactive Socratic Sparring / Execution Quizzes**: The user asks to test their leadership judgment on execution friction, behavioral incentives, or closing the knowing-doing gap in realistic workplace scenarios via 5-question quiz batches.

## Operating Modes

### Mode 1: Active Note Dictation & Continuous Capture

When the user is reading and sharing insights:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Continuous Capture Rule**:
> - Any insight, reflection, or concept shared by the reader while reading is immediately structured and persisted into [reading-notes.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/reading-notes.md).
> - Do not ask for confirmation before saving; proactively capture the insight, enrich it with the book's mechanisms and citations, and report what was recorded.

#### Protocol:
1. Ingest the user's input/reflection.
2. Structure the entry using the standardized format:
   - **Core Insight**: The reader's principle or observation.
   - **Mechanism**: The organizational/cognitive reason why this gap occurs and how action addresses it.
   - **Mindset & Strategic Application**: Concrete implications for leadership, operational habits, and decision-making.
   - **Notable Excerpt**: Direct or contextual quote from Pfeffer & Sutton.
3. Automatically append the entry directly to [reading-notes.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/reading-notes.md).

### Mode 2: Concept Clarification & Book Inquiries

When the user asks questions about specific ideas or case studies in *The Knowing-Doing Gap*:
1. Cross-reference the inquiry with [knowing-doing-framework.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/knowing-doing-framework.md).
2. Clarify the root causes:
   - Does this stem from the "Smart Talk Trap" (conflating talking with doing)?
   - Does it stem from mindless precedent (memory replacing thinking)?
   - Is fear driving risk avoidance and error concealment?
   - Are conflicting measurement systems incentivizing perverse behaviors?
   - Is destructive internal competition blocking knowledge sharing?
3. Provide concrete operational examples and highlight the practical leadership remedy.
4. If the user finds the takeaway valuable, ask: *"Would you like me to capture this takeaway in your reading notes?"*

---

### Mode 3: Organizational Diagnostic & Root-Cause Audit

When the user presents an existing workplace friction, stalled strategy, or team bottleneck:
1. **Audit for the 5 Primary Traps**:
   - *Talk vs. Action*: Are decisions, slide decks, and committees treated as the end-state?
   - *Memory vs. Thinking*: Is the team reflexively doing what was done in the past without questioning relevance?
   - *Fear & Psychological Safety*: Are people afraid to make errors, speak up, or try unproven ideas?
   - *Measurement Distortion*: Are vanity metrics, activity tracking, or short-term numbers overshadowing the real work?
   - *Internal Competition*: Are teams hoarding knowledge or optimizing local sub-goals at the expense of enterprise execution?
2. **Prescribe Actionable Interventions**:
   - Recommend small, low-risk experiments (action-first learning).
   - Define clear follow-ups with accountable doers and rapid feedback loops.
   - Design mechanisms to reward execution and learning from failure rather than rewarding mere talk.

### Mode 4: Socratic Sparring Partner & 5-Question Quizzer

Each quiz session contains exactly 5 questions delivered in a single prompt. It tracks history in [quizzes/](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/README.md) to prevent repeating scenarios and measure improvement over time.

```
[ 1. Review Quiz History ]  -->  [ 2. Select 5 Principles/Notes ]  -->  [ 3. Present 5 Scenarios ]  -->  [ 4. Await User Answers ]  -->  [ 5. Evaluate All 5 ]  -->  [ 6. Save Quiz Log ]
```

#### Step 1: Review Quiz History
Inspect the [knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/README.md) folder:
1. Read existing quiz markdown files (`knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/YYYY-MM-DD-*.md`).
2. Catalog previously tested barriers, guidelines, case studies, and past blind spots.
3. Select 5 fresh execution principles or notes that have not been tested recently, or re-test traps where past quizzes showed gaps. Never repeat the exact same scenario.

#### Step 2: Select 5 Principles or Notes
Choose 5 distinct topics across:
* [reading-notes.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/reading-notes.md) (the reader's captured reflections, case studies, and insights)
* [knowing-doing-framework.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/knowing-doing-framework.md) (the 5 barriers and 8 guidelines)

Ensure the 5 questions span distinct execution traps (e.g., smart talk trap, memory substituting for thinking, fear-driven concealment, metric distortion, destructive internal competition).

#### Step 3: Present 5 Questions in One Batch
Present all 5 questions together in a single message. Do not reveal the underlying principle names or answers upfront. Format each question clearly:

* **Question 1**: Realistic operational dilemma (e.g., executive paralysis via endless slides, fear of post-mortem blame, misaligned ranking metrics).
* **Question 2**: Realistic operational dilemma.
* **Question 3**: Realistic operational dilemma.
* **Question 4**: Realistic operational dilemma.
* **Question 5**: Realistic operational dilemma.

End the batch with instructions for the user:
*"Reply with your answers to questions 1 through 5. Once submitted, I will evaluate your diagnoses, highlight blind spots, link reference principles and reading notes, and record the session in your quiz history so you can mark this task complete."*

#### Step 4: Await the User's Response
Stop and let the user answer all 5 questions in their own words.

#### Step 5: Evaluate All 5 Answers
When the user replies, evaluate each question systematically:

For each question (1 to 5):
1. **Model Answer (Recommended Course of Action)**: The concrete leadership and operational action an experienced operator would take, directly grounded in the book's principles and reading notes.
2. **Comparative Rating vs Model Answer**: Concrete rating of how the user's answer compares to the model answer (e.g., `Strong Alignment (90%)`, `Partial Alignment (60%)`, or `Divergent (30%)`), detailing what the user diagnosed accurately, where their plan diverged, and what execution friction the model answer accounted for.
3. **🎯 Strengths & Alignment**: What the user diagnosed correctly and their sound execution instincts.
4. **🔍 Blind Spots & Hidden Traps**: Subtle systemic traps, perverse incentives, or implementation risks they missed.
5. **📖 Reference Principle / Reading Note**: Link directly to the underlying principle or captured note:
   * e.g., `[Barrier 1: When Talk Substitutes for Action](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/knowing-doing-framework.md#1-when-talk-substitutes-for-action)`
   * e.g., `[Chapter 1 Note: Surface Mechanics vs Deep Philosophy](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/references/reading-notes.md#5-chapter-1-surface-mechanics-vs-deep-philosophy--the-andon-cord-principle)`
6. **💡 Rule of Thumb / Heuristic**: A memorable 1-2 sentence heuristic.
7. **Verdict**: Mark as `Mastered`, `Partially Mastered`, or `Gap Identified`.

Then provide an **Overall Quiz Summary**:
* **Score**: Numerical score (e.g., `4/5`).
* **Progress & Improvement**: Compare against past attempts recorded in [quizzes/](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/README.md) (e.g., whether previously identified blind spots were resolved).
* **Key Learnings**: 2-3 actionable lessons reinforced in this session.

#### Step 6: Persist Quiz Log to `knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/`
Immediately write a new log file to `knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/YYYY-MM-DD-quiz-<topic-slug>.md` using the schema in [knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/README.md](file:///Users/neerav/Documents/Projects/skills/knowing-doing-gap/quizzes/README.md).
The log file must record:
- Date, Quiz ID, Topics tested.
- Overall Score and Improvement Notes.
- All 5 questions, the user's raw answers, evaluations, and principle links.
- Key takeaways and items to revisit.

Notify the user that the quiz is complete and provide a clickable markdown link to the newly generated quiz log file so they can mark their task complete.

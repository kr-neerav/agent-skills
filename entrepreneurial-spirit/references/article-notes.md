# Entrepreneurial Spirit: Article Notes & Discussion Insights Log

This document is the dedicated chronological repository for articles, artifacts, and founder notes analyzed through the entrepreneurial mindset.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Capture Protocol**:
> - Every article shared by the user goes through the two-phase workflow: intake/operator decomposition, sparring debate, and final synthesized logging.
> - Entries must strictly capture concrete mechanics, economic logic, contrarian angles, and operator rules of thumb.

When appending new article entries, use this exact structure:

```markdown
### [Index]. [Article Title]
* **Source**: [URL or Source Citation]
* **Date Analyzed**: YYYY-MM-DD
* **Core Entrepreneurial Thesis**: [1-2 sentences capturing the main business insight]

#### Operator Dimension Breakdown
* **Opportunity Discovery & Customer Pull**: [Hair-on-fire problem identified, customer demand signals, market timing]
* **Distribution & GTM Mechanics**: [Customer acquisition channels, repeatable funnels, sales cycle friction]
* **Unit Economics & Margin Engine**: [Gross margins, CAC payback, pricing power, revenue sustainability]
* **Asymmetric Risk & Downside Exposure**: [Downside limits, failure scenarios, capital requirements, upside multiplier]
* **Execution Velocity & Feedback Loops**: [Speed of iteration, Type 1 vs Type 2 decisions, validation speed]
* **Defensibility & Moats**: [Network effects, switching costs, scale advantages, cornered resources, process power]

#### Sparring Debate & Discussion Takeaways
* **Dilemma Debated**: [Core trade-off or counter-argument explored during discussion]
* **Operator Takeaway**: [Synthesized conclusion reached from the sparring session]

#### Actionable Heuristics
1. [Memorable rule of thumb 1]
2. [Memorable rule of thumb 2]
```
